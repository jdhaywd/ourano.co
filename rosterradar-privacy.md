## 1. What Information Do We Collect?

### Personal information you disclose to us

**In Short:** We collect only the operational aviation data you choose to enter into the app. For verified British Airways users, we also collect your verified airline email address and aggregate usage statistics.

Charlie does not require an account, login, or any personal identification. We do not collect your name, employee number, or any other personal identifiers beyond those described below.

The following categories of data may be entered by you and stored within the app:

- **Airline and fleet preferences** — your airline name and aircraft type, entered during onboarding and stored locally on your device
- **Flight data** — callsigns, flight numbers, routes, departure and arrival times, fuel figures, MSA data, maneuver speeds, crew rest periods, and other operational planning data you enter for each flight
- **Operational flight plan (OFP) data** — figures and documents you import from your airline's OFP, stored locally and in your private iCloud
- **Airport notes and data** — personal notes, visit history, and operational information you record for each airport
- **Satellite imagery** — snapshots of airport locations fetched from Apple Maps and stored on your device for offline reference
- **Personal notes** — text, images, procedures, mnemonics, and tables you create within the app
- **Checklists** — checklist templates and per-flight checklists you build and manage
- **Reminders** — titles, dates, and notification preferences for local notifications you configure
- **Timers** — names and durations for countdown timers you create

### Information collected from verified airline users

If you select British Airways as your airline and choose to complete airline verification, the following additional data is processed:

- **Verified airline email address** — your @ba.com email address, submitted during the verification process and sent to our verification service and backend database. Verification is optional: you may skip it, in which case none of the data below is collected.
- **Usage statistics** — on each app launch after verification, the following aggregate statistics are sent to our servers: your verified email address, a timestamp of your last activity, total app launch count, total number of flights, notes, and airports recorded in the app. These are aggregate counts only — the content of your flights, notes, or airports is never transmitted.

**Sensitive Information.** We do not process any sensitive categories of personal information.

**Information NOT Collected.** We want to be clear about what we do not collect:

- We do not serve advertisements
- We do not sell your data to third parties
- We do not collect GPS location data
- We do not access your contacts, photos library, camera, or microphone
- We do not collect your name, employee number, or personal credentials beyond the airline email address used for verification
- Usage statistics are only collected for users who complete airline verification; unverified users have no data sent to our servers

### Information automatically collected

**In Short:** We do not automatically collect personal information from users who have not completed airline verification.

When you view an airport in the app, the app may request satellite imagery from Apple's MapKit service using the airport's publicly known latitude and longitude coordinates. This is a standard Apple system call and does not transmit any personally identifiable information. Apple's privacy policy governs how Apple handles these requests.

For unverified users, we do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, usage statistics, or any other telemetry data.

## 2. How Do We Process Your Information?

**In Short:** Your operational data stays on your device and in your personal iCloud account. For verified British Airways users, aggregate usage statistics are also sent to our backend on each app launch.

We process your data within the app, through Apple's iCloud infrastructure (which you control through your Apple ID), and — for verified users — through our own backend. We use your data for the following purposes:

- **To provide the Services.** We use the data you enter to power the app's features: flight planning, fuel management, airport reference, notes, checklists, reminders, and timers.
- **To sync across your devices.** If you are signed into iCloud, your data is synchronised to your other Apple devices via your private iCloud database.
- **To back up your data.** With your action, the app can export a backup to your iCloud Drive, which is accessible only to you.
- **To send local notifications.** If you set reminders or timers, the app schedules local notifications on your device. These are generated entirely on-device and do not involve any server or third-party notification service.
- **To verify airline association (verified users only).** If you select British Airways and choose to verify, your @ba.com email address is submitted to our verification service to confirm your association with the airline. This unlocks airline-specific features and settings.
- **To record aggregate usage statistics (verified users only).** On each app launch, aggregate counts of your app activity (launches, flights, notes, airports) are sent to our servers alongside your verified email address and a last-seen timestamp. This helps us understand how the app is used by verified crew.

## 4. When and With Whom Do We Share Your Personal Information?

**In Short:** For most users, your data is not shared with us or with any third party. Verified British Airways users' email addresses and aggregate usage statistics are sent to our own backend. All other data is stored on your device and in your personal iCloud account.

Charlie stores most data exclusively on your device and in your personal iCloud account. For verified users, some data also flows to our own backend. Your data interacts only with the following services:

### System Service Providers

| Provider | Data Shared | Purpose |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| **Apple iCloud** | All app data you enter (flights, airports, notes, checklists, etc.) | Syncing data across your personal Apple devices via your private iCloud account |
| **Apple MapKit** | Airport latitude and longitude (publicly available data) | Fetching satellite imagery of airport locations for offline reference |
| **Apple Notifications** | Reminder titles and scheduled times | Scheduling local notifications on your device |
| **Our backend** (verified users only) | Verified airline email address, last-seen timestamp, and aggregate counts of app launches, flights, notes, and airports | Airline verification and aggregate usage statistics for verified crew |

Apple system services are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Our backend is operated by us and governed by this Privacy Policy.

### We Do NOT Share Data With

- Advertising networks
- Data brokers
- Marketing partners
- Analytics providers unrelated to the app's own usage statistics
- Any other third parties not listed above

## 7. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

**In Short:** Your operational data is protected by Apple's security infrastructure. Verified user statistics are stored in our backend, which uses industry-standard encryption.

The security of your operational data is governed by Apple's security infrastructure, which includes:

- **iOS Data Protection** — data at rest on your device is encrypted using your device passcode
- **iCloud encryption** — data in transit to and from iCloud is encrypted via HTTPS; data stored in iCloud private databases is encrypted at rest by Apple

For verified users, aggregate usage statistics are stored in our database. Encryption in transit (TLS) and encryption at rest is used. Access to this database is restricted to us and is not shared with third parties.

We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. However, no technology can be guaranteed 100% secure. You should protect your device and iCloud account with a strong passcode and two-factor authentication.

## 9. What Are Your Privacy Rights?

**In Short:** In some regions, such as the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom (UK), and Switzerland, you have rights that allow you greater access to and control over your personal information. You may review, change, or delete your data at any time.

In some regions (like the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), you have certain rights under applicable data protection laws. These may include the right (i) to request access and obtain a copy of your personal information, (ii) to request rectification or erasure, (iii) to restrict the processing of your personal information, (iv) if applicable, to data portability, and (v) not to be subject to automated decision-making. In certain circumstances, you may also have the right to object to the processing of your personal information.

You can make such a request by contacting us at privacy@ouranoco.com. We will consider and act upon any request in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

Because most of your data is stored on your own device and iCloud account, you can exercise most of these rights directly:

- **Access and portability:** Export your airport data via the Share button in the Airports tab, or access the iCloud Drive backup file directly in the Files app.
- **Erasure (device and iCloud):** Use Settings → Clear Local Data to delete all data from your device, or Settings → Clear iCloud Data to wipe your iCloud records.
- **Erasure of verified user statistics:** Contact us at privacy@ouranoco.com to request deletion of your email address and usage statistics from our backend. We will process such requests within a reasonable timeframe in accordance with applicable law.

If you are located in the EEA or UK and you believe we are unlawfully processing your personal information, you also have the right to complain to your [Member State data protection authority](https://ec.europa.eu/justice/data-protection/bodies/authorities/index_en.htm) or [UK data protection authority](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/data-protection-complaints/data-protection-complaints/).

If you are located in Switzerland, you may contact the [Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/edoeb/en/home.html).

**Withdrawing your consent:** If we are relying on your consent to process your personal information, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. You can withdraw your consent by contacting us at privacy@ouranoco.com or by deleting the app and its associated iCloud data. To withdraw consent for the processing of your verified email and usage statistics, contact us at privacy@ouranoco.com and we will remove your record from our backend. However, please note that this will not affect the lawfulness of the processing before its withdrawal nor will it affect the processing of your personal information conducted in reliance on lawful processing grounds other than consent.

### Data Deletion

As Charlie does not use a standard account system, there is no account to delete. To remove all your data:

- Use **Settings → Clear Local Data** to delete all data on this device
- Use **Settings → Clear iCloud Data** to delete all synced records from iCloud
- Delete iCloud Drive backups manually from the **Files app**
- Delete the app from your device to remove any remaining local data
- Contact **privacy@ouranoco.com** to request deletion of your verified email address and usage statistics from our backend (verified users only)

## 10. Controls for Do-Not-Track Features

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems and mobile applications include a Do-Not-Track ('DNT') feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognising and implementing DNT signals has been finalised. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or any other mechanism that automatically communicates your choice not to be tracked online. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we must follow in the future, we will inform you about that practice in a revised version of this Privacy Policy.

Charlie does not track your online browsing activities and does not use any advertising or third-party tracking technologies. Usage statistics sent to our backend are limited to aggregate counts and are used solely for understanding app engagement among verified crew.
