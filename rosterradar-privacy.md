## 1. What Information Do We Collect?

### Personal information you disclose to us

**In Short:** We do not collect any personal information. The only data stored is kept locally on your device and is never transmitted to us.

Roster Radar does not require an account, login, or any personal identification with us. The following data is stored locally on your device:

- **Staff number** — your six-digit British Airways staff number, saved locally on your device so you do not need to re-enter it each time you open the app.
- **Cached roster data** — flight trip data retrieved from British Airways' eMaestro system (trip identifiers, flight numbers, routes, departure and arrival times) is cached locally on your device so it can be displayed without a network request on subsequent opens.

### Information entered at runtime but not stored

- **BSAFE password** — when eMaestro requires authentication, you are prompted to enter your BSAFE password. This password is used solely to authenticate with British Airways' own systems and is **never written to storage** of any kind by this app. It exists only in memory for the duration of the authentication request and is discarded immediately afterwards.

### Information NOT collected

We want to be clear about what we do not collect:

- We do not collect any data on our own servers — no data from this app is ever sent to Ouranoco.
- We do not serve advertisements.
- We do not sell your data to third parties.
- We do not collect GPS location data.
- We do not access your contacts, photos library, camera, or microphone.
- We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, usage statistics, or any telemetry data.
- We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

### Information automatically collected

**In Short:** We do not automatically collect any information. The app does not transmit any data to us.

## 2. How Do We Process Your Information?

**In Short:** Your staff number and roster cache are stored only on your device. We do not process your information in any way.

All data the app stores is held in local device storage (iOS UserDefaults) and never leaves your device to reach our servers. We use your data for the following purposes, all of which occur entirely on-device:

- **To provide the Services.** Your staff number is saved locally so you do not have to re-enter it each time. Your cached roster data is stored locally so it can be displayed on subsequent app opens without requiring a new network request.
- **To retrieve your roster from British Airways.** When you tap "Look Up Roster", the app sends your staff number (and, if required, your password) directly to British Airways' eMaestro system at `egprd.baplc.com`. This communication is between your device and British Airways' servers — it does not pass through any Ouranoco infrastructure.

Roster Radar does not route any user data through Ouranoco infrastructure. Your data interacts only with the following:

| Recipient | Data Shared | Purpose |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **British Airways eMaestro** (`egprd.baplc.com`) | Staff number, BSAFE password (when authentication is required) | Authenticating with BA's systems and retrieving your roster on your behalf |

British Airways' systems are governed by British Airways' own privacy policy. We do not have visibility into, or control over, how British Airways processes your credentials on their servers.

### We Do NOT Share Data With

- Advertising networks
- Data brokers
- Marketing partners
- Analytics providers
- Any other third parties not listed above

## 5. Does the App Require an Account or Login?

Roster Radar does not have a user account system. You do not need to create an account with us, provide a username, or sign in with us to use the app. The authentication that takes place is between your device and British Airways' servers, using your existing BA staff credentials.

Your BSAFE password is entered by you when prompted and is used only to authenticate with BA's eMaestro system. It is never stored by the app.

## 7. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

**In Short:** Your data is protected by iOS security infrastructure. The app uses certificate pinning when communicating with British Airways' servers.

The security of your data is governed by Apple's iOS security infrastructure, which includes:

- **iOS Data Protection** — data at rest on your device is encrypted using your device passcode.
- **Certificate pinning** — when the app communicates with British Airways' eMaestro servers, it uses certificate pinning to verify the server's identity and prevent man-in-the-middle attacks.

Because no data is transmitted to Ouranoco's servers, there is no Ouranoco-side data storage that could be compromised. You should protect your device with a strong passcode and ensure your BSAFE credentials are kept secure in accordance with British Airways' own security guidelines.
