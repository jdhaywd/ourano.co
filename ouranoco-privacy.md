# Ouranoco Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 31, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Ouranoco ('Ouranoco', 'we', 'us', or 'our') accesses, collects, stores, uses, and/or shares ('processes') your personal information when you use any of our apps and services ('Services'), including when you:

- Download and use any of our mobile apps, or any other app of ours that links to this Privacy Policy,
- Engage with us in other related ways, including any sales, marketing, or events.

This is our **company-wide policy**. It applies to every Ouranoco app and sets out the commitments, legal bases, and rights that are common to all of them.

Because our apps do very different things, each one also has its own **app-specific privacy notice** that sets out exactly what that app collects, where it goes, who processes it, and how long it is kept. **You should read this policy together with the notice for the app you use.** Where an app-specific notice conflicts with this policy, the app-specific notice governs for that app.

## 1. Who We Are and What This Policy Covers

**In Short:** Ouranoco is the data controller for our apps. This policy covers all of them; each app also has its own notice with the specifics.

Ouranoco produces mobile applications for airline crew. For the personal information described in this policy and in our app-specific notices, Ouranoco is the **data controller** — meaning we are responsible for deciding how and why that information is processed.

This policy applies to every Ouranoco app, including Charlie, FlightLine, and Roster Radar, and to any future app that links to it.

**How this policy fits together with the app notices.** This policy tells you the things that are true across all our apps: our commitments, our legal bases, your rights, and how to contact us. ## 2. Our Privacy Commitments

**In Short:** These commitments apply to every Ouranoco app, without exception.

Across all of our Services:

- **We do not sell or rent your personal information** to anyone, for any purpose.
- **We do not serve advertisements** in any of our apps.
- **We do not share your personal information with advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing partners.**
- **We do not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs** into any of our apps.
- **We do not collect GPS location data.**
- **We do not access your contacts, photo library, camera, or microphone.**
- **We do not build advertising profiles, and we do not use your data for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.**
- **We do not use your personal information to train machine learning models.**
- **We collect the minimum data needed** for a feature to work, and we prefer on-device storage over server storage wherever the feature allows it.
- **We never store your airline or employer passwords on our servers.** Where an app authenticates against an airline system, your credentials are either held in memory only for the duration of the request, or stored in your device's encrypted Keychain, as described in that app's notice.

Some of our apps record a small amount of **first-party operational data** — for example, aggregate counts used to understand engagement or to enforce fair usage limits on a paid third-party API. This is not third-party tracking, it is never sold or shared for marketing, and where it happens it is set out in full in the relevant app's notice.

## 3. What Information Do We Collect?

**In Short:** It depends heavily on the app. Some collect nothing on our servers at all.

The categories of personal information we may process across our Services include:

- **Employment and crew identifiers** — such as a staff number, crew code, or airline email address, used to authenticate you with your airline's systems or to verify your association with an airline.
- **Operational and roster data** — such as rosters, duties, sectors, flights, flight plan figures, airport information, and related notes that you enter or import.
- **Account and profile data** — such as a display name, authentication identifier, or subscription status, where an app has an account system.
- **Configuration and preference data** — such as reminders, checklists, timers, key dates, and notification settings.
- **Financial parameters you choose to enter** — such as salary and tax details entered for pay estimation. Where an app offers this, the data is processed **on your device only** and is never transmitted to us.
- **Limited service telemetry** — such as a last-seen timestamp or a count of API calls, where needed to operate or fairly limit a feature.

**Sensitive information.** We do not process special category data as defined by the UK GDPR and GDPR. Where an app handles financial information you have voluntarily entered, that processing takes place entirely on your device.

**We do not receive personal information about you from third-party data sources.** Where an app retrieves information from your airline's systems, it does so at your instruction and using your own credentials.

**The definitive inventory for your app is in its app-specific notice.** Please refer to the notice for [Charlie](/charlie-privacy), [FlightLine](/flightline-privacy), or [Roster Radar](/privacy/rosterradar).

## 4. How Do We Process Your Information?

**In Short:** Only to provide, maintain, secure, and support the app you are using.

We process personal information for the following purposes:

- **To provide the Services.** To deliver the features of the app you have chosen to use.

- **To comply with our legal obligations.**

We do not process your personal information for any purpose that is incompatible with those listed above, and we will not use it for a materially new purpose without first informing you and, where required, obtaining your consent.

## 5. What Legal Bases Do We Rely On to Process Your Personal Information?

**In Short:** We only process your personal information when we believe it is necessary and we have a valid legal reason to do so under applicable law.

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR require us to explain the valid legal bases we rely on in order to process your personal information. We may rely on the following legal bases:

- **Consent.** We may process your information if you have given us permission to use your personal information for a specific purpose. You can withdraw your consent at any time. [Learn more about withdrawing your consent](#11-what-are-your-privacy-rights).
- **Performance of a Contract.** We may process your personal information when we believe it is necessary to fulfil our contractual obligations to you, including providing our Services or at your request prior to entering into a contract with you.
- **Legitimate Interests.** We may process your information where it is necessary for our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the Services, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms.
- **Legal Obligations.** We may process your information where we believe it is necessary for compliance with our legal obligations, such as to cooperate with a law enforcement body or regulatory agency, exercise or defend our legal rights, or disclose your information as evidence in litigation in which we are involved.
- **Vital Interests.** We may process your information where we believe it is necessary to protect your vital interests or the vital interests of a third party, such as situations involving potential threats to the safety of any person.

Where an app stores data only on your own device and transmits nothing to us, these bases apply only to that local processing, and in practice there is no server-side processing by us to justify.

## 8. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

**In Short:** Only as long as needed for the purpose we collected it — and for much of our data, retention is entirely under your control.

We will only keep your personal information for as long as it is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Because much of the data our apps handle is stored **on your own device** or in **your own iCloud account**, its retention is under your direct control: you can delete it in-app or by deleting the app.

Where we do hold information on our servers, we retain it for as long as you maintain your account or record with us, and we delete or anonymise it from our active databases when you ask us to or when it is no longer needed. Some information may persist in backup archives for a limited period, during which it is securely isolated from any further processing until deletion is possible.

**Per-data-type retention tables are set out in each app's notice.**

## 9. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

**In Short:** We apply a security baseline across every app, plus additional measures specific to each one.

We have implemented appropriate and reasonable technical and organisational security measures designed to protect the personal information we process. Across all of our apps this baseline includes:

- **iOS Data Protection** — data stored at rest on your device is encrypted using your device passcode.
- **Encryption in transit** — all network communication uses HTTPS/TLS.
- **iOS Keychain** — any credentials stored on your device are held in the system's encrypted Keychain.
- **No storage of airline passwords on our servers** — where an app authenticates against an employer system, credentials are used in memory only or kept in your device's Keychain.
- **Data minimisation** — we default to on-device storage and collect the least data a feature can work with.
- **Restricted access** — where we do operate a database, access is limited to those who need it, and it is not shared with third parties.
- **No third-party tracking SDKs** — reducing the number of parties that could ever be exposed to your data.

Individual apps apply further protections — such as certificate pinning, Sign in with Apple, or reliance on Apple's private iCloud database — as described in their notices.

However, despite our safeguards, no electronic transmission over the Internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, so we cannot promise or guarantee that hackers, cybercriminals, or other unauthorised third parties will not be able to defeat our security and improperly collect, access, steal, or modify your information. Transmission of personal information to and from our Services is at your own risk. You should access the Services only within a secure environment, and protect your device and any linked accounts with a strong passcode and two-factor authentication.

## 11. What Are Your Privacy Rights?

**In Short:** In some regions, such as the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom (UK), and Switzerland, you have rights that allow you greater access to and control over your personal information.

In some regions (like the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), you have certain rights under applicable data protection laws. These may include the right (i) to request access and obtain a copy of your personal information, (ii) to request rectification or erasure, (iii) to restrict the processing of your personal information, (iv) if applicable, to data portability, and (v) not to be subject to automated decision-making. In certain circumstances, you may also have the right to object to the processing of your personal information.

You can make such a request by contacting us at privacy@ouranoco.com. We will consider and act upon any request in accordance with applicable data protection laws, and will respond within one month of receipt, as required by the UK GDPR and GDPR.

**Exercising your rights directly.** For several of our apps, your data is stored on your own device or in your own iCloud account rather than on our servers. In those cases you can exercise most of these rights yourself, immediately, without contacting us — your app's notice explains exactly how. Where we hold no information about you at all, there is nothing for you to request from us.

**Withdrawing your consent.** If we are relying on your consent to process your personal information, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. You can withdraw your consent by contacting us at privacy@ouranoco.com, or, for apps that store data locally, by deleting the app and its associated data. Withdrawing consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before its withdrawal, nor will it affect processing conducted in reliance on lawful grounds other than consent.

**Complaints.** If you are located in the EEA or UK and you believe we are unlawfully processing your personal information, you also have the right to complain to your [Member State data protection authority](https://ec.europa.eu/justice/data-protection/bodies/authorities/index_en.htm) or [UK data protection authority](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/data-protection-complaints/data-protection-complaints/).

If you are located in Switzerland, you may contact the [Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/edoeb/en/home.html).

## 12. Controls for Do-Not-Track Features

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems and mobile applications include a Do-Not-Track ('DNT') feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognising and implementing DNT signals has been finalised. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or any other mechanism that automatically communicates your choice not to be tracked online. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we must follow in the future, we will inform you about that practice in a revised version of this Privacy Policy.

In practice, no Ouranoco app tracks your online browsing activities, and none use advertising or third-party tracking technologies.
