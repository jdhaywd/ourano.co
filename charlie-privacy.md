# Charlie Privacy Notice

**Last updated: August 31, 2026**

> **Read this together with the [Ouranoco Privacy Policy](/privacy).**
>
> The [Ouranoco Privacy Policy](/privacy) is our company-wide policy. It covers our privacy commitments, the legal bases we rely on, your rights under the UK GDPR and GDPR, international transfers, how to complain, and how to contact us. It applies in full to Charlie.
>
> **This notice covers only what is specific to Charlie**: exactly what data the app handles, where it is stored, who else is involved, how long it is kept, and how to delete it. Where this notice and the company policy differ, this notice governs for Charlie.

Charlie is an operational tool for airline flight crew. This notice describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ('process') your personal information when you download and use the Charlie mobile app.

Questions or concerns? Contact us at privacy@ouranoco.com.

---

## Charlie at a Glance

- **Does Charlie require an account?** No. There is no account, login, or personal identification required to use the app.
- **What does Ouranoco receive?** For most users, **nothing at all**. If you are a British Airways user and you choose to complete the optional airline verification, we receive your verified @ba.com email address and a small set of aggregate usage counts.
- **Where does everything else live?** On your device, and — if you are signed into iCloud — in your own private iCloud database. We cannot see it.
- **Sensitive information?** None is processed.
- **Third-party analytics, ads, or trackers?** None.
- **How do you delete your data?** In-app, via Settings → Clear Local Data and Settings → Clear iCloud Data. Verified users can also email privacy@ouranoco.com to have their backend record removed.

## A1. What Information Does Charlie Collect?

**In Short:** Charlie collects only the operational aviation data you choose to enter into the app, which stays on your device and in your own iCloud. For verified British Airways users, we additionally receive a verified airline email address and aggregate usage counts.

### Data you enter, stored on your device and in your iCloud

Charlie does not require an account, login, or any personal identification. We do not collect your name, employee number, or any other personal identifiers beyond those described below.

The following categories of data may be entered by you and stored within the app:

- **Airline and fleet preferences** — your airline name and aircraft type, entered during onboarding and stored locally on your device
- **Flight data** — callsigns, flight numbers, routes, departure and arrival times, fuel figures, MSA data, maneuver speeds, crew rest periods, and other operational planning data you enter for each flight
- **Operational flight plan (OFP) data** — figures and documents you import from your airline's OFP, stored locally and in your private iCloud
- **Airport notes and data** — personal notes, visit history, and operational information you record for each airport
- **Satellite imagery** — snapshots of airport locations fetched from Apple Maps and stored on your device for offline reference
- **Personal notes** — text, images, procedures, mnemonics, and tables you create within the app
- **Checklists** — checklist templates and per-flight checklists you build and manage
- **Reminders** — titles, dates, and notification preferences for local notifications you configure
- **Timers** — names and durations for countdown timers you create

None of the above is transmitted to Ouranoco.

### Data collected from verified airline users

If you select British Airways as your airline and choose to complete airline verification, the following additional data is processed **by us**:

- **Verified airline email address** — your @ba.com email address, submitted during the verification process and sent to our verification service and backend database. **Verification is optional**: you may skip it, in which case none of the data below is collected.
- **Usage statistics** — on each app launch after verification, the following aggregate statistics are sent to our servers: your verified email address, a timestamp of your last activity, total app launch count, and total number of flights, notes, and airports recorded in the app. These are **aggregate counts only** — the content of your flights, notes, or airports is never transmitted.

These counts are first-party operational statistics used solely to understand engagement among verified crew. They are not third-party analytics, and they are never sold or shared. See [Our Privacy Commitments](/privacy#2-our-privacy-commitments).

### Sensitive information

Charlie does not process any sensitive or special category personal information.

### Information automatically collected

**For unverified users, Charlie collects nothing automatically.** We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, usage statistics, or any other telemetry.

When you view an airport in the app, the app may request satellite imagery from Apple's MapKit service using the airport's publicly known latitude and longitude coordinates. This is a standard Apple system call and does not transmit any personally identifiable information. Apple's privacy policy governs how Apple handles these requests.

### Information NOT collected

In addition to the [company-wide commitments](/privacy#2-our-privacy-commitments) — no ads, no data sales, no GPS, no contacts/photos/camera/microphone access, no third-party tracking SDKs — we want to be specific about Charlie:

- We do not collect your name, employee number, or personal credentials beyond the airline email address used for verification
- Usage statistics are collected **only** for users who complete airline verification; unverified users have no data sent to our servers whatsoever
- We never receive the content of your flights, notes, airports, checklists, or OFP data

## A2. How Does Charlie Use Your Information?

**In Short:** Your operational data stays on your device and in your personal iCloud account. For verified British Airways users, aggregate usage statistics are also sent to our backend on each app launch.

We process your data within the app, through Apple's iCloud infrastructure (which you control through your Apple ID), and — for verified users only — through our own backend:

- **To provide the Services.** We use the data you enter to power the app's features: flight planning, fuel management, airport reference, notes, checklists, reminders, and timers.
- **To sync across your devices.** If you are signed into iCloud, your data is synchronised to your other Apple devices via your private iCloud database.
- **To back up your data.** With your action, the app can export a backup to your iCloud Drive, which is accessible only to you.
- **To send local notifications.** If you set reminders or timers, the app schedules local notifications on your device. These are generated entirely on-device and do not involve any server or third-party notification service.
- **To verify airline association (verified users only).** If you select British Airways and choose to verify, your @ba.com email address is submitted to our verification service to confirm your association with the airline. This unlocks airline-specific features and settings.
- **To record aggregate usage statistics (verified users only).** On each app launch, aggregate counts of your app activity (launches, flights, notes, airports) are sent to our servers alongside your verified email address and a last-seen timestamp. This helps us understand how the app is used by verified crew.

The legal bases we rely on for this processing are set out in the [Ouranoco Privacy Policy](/privacy#5-what-legal-bases-do-we-rely-on-to-process-your-personal-information). Airline verification and the associated usage statistics are processed on the basis of your **consent**, which you give by choosing to verify and can withdraw at any time.

## A3. Who Is Your Information Shared With?

**In Short:** For most users, your data is not shared with us or with any third party. Verified British Airways users' email addresses and aggregate usage counts go to our own backend and nowhere else.

Charlie stores most data exclusively on your device and in your personal iCloud account. Your data interacts only with the following:

| Recipient | Data involved | Purpose |
|-----------|---------------|---------|
| **Apple iCloud** | All app data you enter (flights, airports, notes, checklists, etc.) | Syncing data across your personal Apple devices via your private iCloud account |
| **Apple MapKit** | Airport latitude and longitude (publicly available data) | Fetching satellite imagery of airport locations for offline reference |
| **Apple Notifications** | Reminder titles and scheduled times | Scheduling local notifications on your device |
| **Ouranoco backend** *(verified users only)* | Verified airline email address, last-seen timestamp, and aggregate counts of app launches, flights, notes, and airports | Airline verification and aggregate usage statistics for verified crew |

Apple system services are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy — your iCloud data is held in your own private database, which we have no access to. Our backend is operated by us and governed by this notice and the [Ouranoco Privacy Policy](/privacy).

Charlie shares data with **no** advertising networks, data brokers, marketing partners, third-party analytics providers, or any other third party. See [company-wide sharing rules](/privacy#6-when-and-with-whom-do-we-share-your-personal-information).

## A4. Does Charlie Require an Account or Login?

**In Short:** No. Charlie does not require any account, sign-in, or personal credentials.

Charlie has no user account system. You do not need to create an account, provide a username, or sign in to use the app. No personally identifiable information is required to use any feature.

Data sync across your devices is handled entirely by Apple iCloud, using your existing Apple ID. We do not have access to your Apple credentials or iCloud data.

Airline verification for British Airways users is **voluntary**. If you choose to verify, your @ba.com email address is used solely for the purposes described in this notice and is not linked to a user account.

## A5. How Long Is Your Information Kept?

**In Short:** Your operational data is kept for as long as you choose to keep it. Verified user records are retained until you ask us to delete them.

Because almost all of your data is stored on your device and in your personal iCloud account — not on our servers — its retention is entirely under your control.

| Data type | Where stored | How to delete |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| Airline and fleet preferences | Device (local settings) | Settings → Clear Local Data, or delete the app |
| Flight data | Device + iCloud | Delete individual flights in-app, or Settings → Clear iCloud Data |
| Airport data and notes | Device + iCloud | Delete individual airports in-app, or Settings → Clear iCloud Data |
| Personal notes | Device + iCloud | Delete individual notes in-app, or Settings → Clear iCloud Data |
| Checklists and templates | Device + iCloud | Delete in-app, or Settings → Clear iCloud Data |
| Reminders and timers | Device + iCloud | Delete in-app, or Settings → Clear iCloud Data |
| Satellite imagery | Device (local storage) | Cleared when you delete the airport or delete the app |
| iCloud Drive backups | iCloud Drive | Delete manually from the Files app |
| Verified email and usage statistics *(verified users only)* | Ouranoco backend | Email privacy@ouranoco.com to request deletion |

## A6. Charlie-Specific Security Measures

**In Short:** Your operational data is protected by Apple's security infrastructure. The small amount of verified-user data we hold is encrypted in transit and at rest.

In addition to the [company-wide security baseline](/privacy#9-how-do-we-keep-your-information-safe), Charlie relies on:

- **iOS Data Protection** — data at rest on your device is encrypted using your device passcode
- **Apple's private iCloud database** — data in transit to and from iCloud is encrypted via HTTPS, and data stored in iCloud private databases is encrypted at rest by Apple. This is your own iCloud account: Ouranoco has no access to it.
- **Encrypted backend storage (verified users only)** — verified email addresses and aggregate counts are transmitted over TLS and encrypted at rest. Access to this database is restricted to us and is not shared with third parties.
- **No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs** are integrated into the app.

No technology can be guaranteed 100% secure. You should protect your device and iCloud account with a strong passcode and two-factor authentication.

## A7. Deleting Your Data

Charlie has no account system, so there is no account to delete. To remove all of your data:

- Use **Settings → Clear Local Data** to delete all data on this device
- Use **Settings → Clear iCloud Data** to delete all synced records from iCloud
- Delete iCloud Drive backups manually from the **Files app**
- Delete the app from your device to remove any remaining local data
- **Verified users only:** email **privacy@ouranoco.com** to request deletion of your verified email address and usage statistics from our backend

## A8. Exercising Your Rights in Charlie

Your full rights are described in the [Ouranoco Privacy Policy](/privacy#11-what-are-your-privacy-rights). Because most of your Charlie data lives on your own device and iCloud account, you can exercise most of them directly:

- **Access and portability:** Export your airport data via the Share button in the Airports tab, or access the iCloud Drive backup file directly in the Files app.
- **Erasure (device and iCloud):** Use Settings → Clear Local Data, or Settings → Clear iCloud Data.
- **Erasure of verified user statistics:** Email privacy@ouranoco.com to request deletion of your email address and usage statistics from our backend. We will process such requests within one month, in accordance with applicable law.
- **Withdrawing consent to verification:** Email privacy@ouranoco.com and we will remove your record from our backend. You may also delete the app and its associated iCloud data.

If you are unverified, we hold no information about you, so there is nothing on our side to access, port, or delete.

To make any other request, or to complain, see [sections 11 and 14 of the Ouranoco Privacy Policy](/privacy#11-what-are-your-privacy-rights).
